# Public datasets referenced by the study

- **USTC-TFC2016**: traffic classification dataset released by researchers at University of Science and Technology of China, containing normal and malicious traffic. Consult the dataset's original publication and authorized distribution source for licenses and allowed use.
- **Malware Capture Facility Project (MCFP)**: publicly described network traffic captures from the Stratosphere Laboratory research environment. The DRTF-Net manuscript reports results for an MCFP-derived multiclass setting; only the general data source is identified here.

**Not distributed here:** PCAP files, labeled flow tables, graph caches, split indices, prediction outputs, or checkpoints. Dataset access alone is insufficient for independent reproduction of the study's exact numerical results.
